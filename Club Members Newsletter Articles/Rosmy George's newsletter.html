Growing with Toastmasters

Joining Toastmasters has been a transformative experience in my personal and professional journey. I joined Toastmasters because of TM Kalpana Arvind, whom I met randomly one evening. She invited me to a Toastmasters meeting happening near my community, and I decided to attend because of the warm welcome I received from her. When I first attended the meeting, I was hesitant to speak in front of an audience and often lacked confidence in expressing my ideas clearly. However, the supportive and structured environment helped me gradually overcome these challenges, and the constructive feedback I received improved my clarity, organization, and confidence.

Over time, Toastmasters has helped me become a more effective communicator and a better listener. Taking up different roles during meetings strengthened my leadership skills and sense of responsibility. This growth has positively impacted my professional life, especially in my interactions with clients and colleagues. Beyond skill development, the encouragement from fellow members and the opportunity to continuously improve have made this journey truly rewarding.

Rosmy George
Member
