My new venture

I have been a passionate Toastmaster since February 2026. My journey began when I was introduced to the unique trilingual Dubai Tamil Malayalam Toastmasters Club by Division Director DTM Alwyn Philomen. Wanting to enhance my communication and leadership skills, I decided to visit the club as a guest.

From the moment I arrived, I received a warm welcome from all the members. During the meeting, I was given an opportunity to participate in the impromptu speaking session, known as Table Topics. The Table Topics Master asked the question, “How do you plan your life to make it successful?” Without hesitation, I volunteered, walked to the center of the stage, and began my response by singing a few lines from a famous song from the movie Baashha, starring Superstar Rajinikanth. Since I sang in my mother tongue, Tamil, many members enjoyed it and applauded my speech enthusiastically.

That moment was a turning point for me. I realized that I could speak confidently in front of an audience without fear or hesitation because I felt a strong sense of belonging within that community. Inspired by the experience, I decided to join the club and officially, begin my Toastmasters journey. Soon after, I took on the role of Sergeant at Arms during the club contest, which gave me valuable experience in leadership and event management. I then prepared and delivered my first speech—my Ice Breaker—in front of my fellow members. Their appreciation and standing ovation made the experience truly memorable and boosted my confidence even further.

Today, I look forward to delivering many more speeches, sharing my ideas and experiences, and continuously improving as a communicator and leader. Through Toastmasters, I hope to grow into a more confident, effective, and inspiring speaker in the years to come.

Prakash Gopalakrishnan
