My visits to DTM Club have been truly memorable and enriching. I had the privilege of engaging with remarkable members, including TM Kalpana (President), excom members TM Archana, TM Varun, TM Anumol, TM Shabana, TM Sivaramakrishnan (Star Speaker), TM Antony, and TM Vishwanath. Their warmth, dedication, and passion for learning made every interaction truly memorable.

A special highlight in my journey was conducting the excom Installation, which was my very first responsibility as an Area Director—made even more significant as it was with your esteemed DTM Club.

The Onam celebration at the club remains unforgettable, showcasing vibrant culture, togetherness, and strong member bonding. The Open House event was another remarkable experience, reflecting excellent planning, energy, and enthusiastic participation.

DTM Club has always extended strong support to the Area whenever needed—be it the Tall Tales Contest, Area-level events, or Division-level programs. Your readiness to step forward and contribute truly makes a difference.

I cannot simply say thank you—I extend my heartfelt gratitude to the entire DTM Club. DTM Club truly exemplifies consistency, teamwork, and the true spirit of Toastmasters growth and excellence.

Mahalakshmi Ravindran
Area 21 Director
