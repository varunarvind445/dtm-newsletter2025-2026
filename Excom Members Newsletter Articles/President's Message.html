A Legacy of Excellence - Celebrating our Trilingual Journey

It is an honor and a privilege to serve as the President of this distinguished trilingual Toastmasters club — a club that stands as a testament to cultural pride, linguistic diversity, and shared excellence. I am truly empowered by an exceptional Executive Committee, whose dedication and leadership are the pillars of our club’s success:
  • Vice President Education: TM Archana Rajesh Kumar
  • Vice President Membership: TM Shiva Kandaswamy
  • Vice President Public Relations: TM Varun Arvind
  • Treasurer: TM Shabana Begum
  • Secretary: TM Vishwanath Rajesh Kumar (June 2025-December 2025) & TM Sivaramakrishnan Elumalai (January 2026-June 2026)
  • Sergeant at Arms: TM Anumol Kunnumpurath Appukuttan
With such remarkable leaders by my side, I feel not only supported, but uplifted — my vision is clearer, my responsibility stronger, and my purpose energised. As President, I pledge to give my very best to this club’s continued growth and distinction. I will actively participate in all meetings and contests — not just as a contestant, but as an organiser, collaborator, and cheerleader for every member’s success. Being a proud charter member since the club’s inception in 2020, I take immense pride in being part of a space where I can refine my English eloquence while embracing my mother tongue, Tamil, and engaging with Malayalam — all under one vibrant banner of learning and camaraderie. Together, let’s elevate our club to new heights, build leaders of influence, and create a legacy that speaks powerfully in three languages — and one voice!!!

Kalpana Arvind
President
