My Experience in Toastmasters as a Protégé

“A mentor is someone who allows you to see the hope inside yourself.” — Oprah Winfrey Joining Dubai Malayalam Toastmasters Club has been one of the most meaningful decisions in my personal growth journey. As a relatively new member—just one year into Toastmasters—I often describe myself as a baby taking my first steps, learning, growing, and discovering my potential with the support of this wonderful community. I was encouraged to join the club by our then President, TM Archana. Soon after, I received a call from our current President, TM Kalpana, who has since become my mentor and a guiding force throughout my Toastmasters journey. Her consistent support and encouragement helped me feel connected and confident from the very beginning. What struck me most about Toastmasters is its unique culture—one where members genuinely care about each other’s growth. The willingness to guide, support, and uplift one another is truly inspiring. As a protégé, I deeply appreciated my mentor’s dedication. Through regular messages, calls, and guidance, she made sure I understood the club’s functioning and felt welcomed at every step. From my early days in the club, I was given opportunities to deliver speeches, take up meeting roles, and even serve as an excom member within a short period. This environment of trust and encouragement helped me step out of my comfort zone and grow with confidence. Mentoring also played a key role in helping me choose my pathway and structure my speeches effectively. More importantly, it taught me how to apply these learnings beyond Toastmasters—into my personal and professional life. One of the most valuable lessons I have learned through mentoring is patience. Reflecting on past experiences, I now realize how a calmer and more understanding approach could have changed my reactions and outcomes. Today, this shift is evident not only in my workplace but also in my family life. I have learned that patience does not weaken authority—it strengthens relationships. I have also been fortunate to receive guidance from TM SRK and TM Archana. While I may not always have fully matched their efforts, I am grateful for their support and hope to make better use of such mentorship opportunities in the future. I would like to conclude with a timeless Tamil proverb from Moodhurai by Avvaiyar: “கற்றாரைக் கற்றாரே காமுறுவர்.” It reminds us that true learning is recognized and valued by those who continue to grow and seek knowledge. As I complete Level 2 in my Pathway Presentation Mastery, I extend my heartfelt gratitude to my mentors and fellow members who have supported me along the way. This journey has inspired me to step into leadership with a new perspective—one that values empathy, patience, and service. As Simon Sinek says, “Leadership is not about being in charge. It’s about taking care of those in your charge.” I am now on my leadership journey—and I look forward to growing further while supporting others along the way.

Shabana Begum
Treasurer
