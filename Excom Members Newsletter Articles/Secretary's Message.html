My Journey with Toastmasters

Joining Toastmasters in July 2023 marked the beginning of a significant chapter in my life, a journey that would not only hone my public speaking skills but also foster invaluable relationships and personal growth. Time has flown by since then, and as I completed the “Persuasive Influence” pathway in December 2025, I am filled with gratitude and reflection on the myriad of experiences that have shaped my development.

At first, stepping into that room filled with eager faces was nerve-wracking. The thought of speaking in public often sends shivers down the spine; it is a common fear that many people share. However, I was determined to conquer my trepidation. With each session, I learned that overcoming fear is akin to climbing a mountain; each speech and every constructive feedback session became a stepping stone towards self-confidence.

One of the key tactics I learned was voice clarity. Initially, I often struggled with volume and articulation. Through relentless practice and feedback, I realized that speaking clearly is not just about projecting one’s voice, but also about ensuring that every word carries weight. It’s akin to a painter choosing vibrant colors for their masterpiece; the right emphasis can make all the difference. As time passed, I not only noticed an improvement in my clarity but also in the ability to hold the audience's attention.

Stage coverage was another essential skill I acquired during my time with Toastmasters. Moving around the stage, using gestures, and maintaining eye contact transformed my delivery. I learned that effective communication transcends words; it involves captivating your audience through body language and presence. As the adage goes, “actions speak louder than words.” This became evident as my stage presence grew, inviting my listeners into my narrative.

Developing content with the audience in mind was an eye-opening experience. I learned that effective speakers do not merely deliver a monologue; they engage in a dialogue with their listeners. Tailoring content to the audience's interests and perspectives is like a chef seasoning a dish to suit the guests’ palate. Understanding my audience allowed me to bridge gaps and connect on a deeper level. By employing anecdotes and relatable examples, I learned to weave a narrative that resonated with my listeners, enhancing both engagement and retention.

The leadership skills I developed throughout this journey have been equally transformative. Toastmasters has a unique structure where members assume various roles, cultivating a sense of responsibility and teamwork. I learned that leadership is not solely about having followers; it is about guiding and inspiring others. By taking on roles such as Toastmaster of the Day and evaluator, I was given the opportunity to lead by example. This experience strengthened my ability to provide constructive feedback, enabling me to encourage others and foster an environment of growth and learning.

As I reflect on this journey, I cannot overlook the fantastic network I built along the way. Toastmasters is a melting pot of diverse individuals, each bringing unique perspectives and experiences. The friendships I forged represent the cherry on top of this journey. Whether it was sharing tips after speeches or simply enjoying conversations over coffee, these interactions provided a sense of camaraderie. In a world that often seems disconnected, the bonds I formed at Toastmasters have been a breath of fresh air.

While completing the “Persuasive Influence” pathway was a tangible milestone, the underlying lesson was that growth is a continuous process. The skills I acquired extend beyond the realm of public speaking and leadership. Life, much like public speaking, is about listening, adapting, and connecting. I have learned to embrace challenges and view them as opportunities for growth rather than obstacles.

In conclusion, my experience with Toastmasters has not only sharpened my public speaking abilities but also enriched my life in multifaceted ways. The journey from being an apprehensive speaker to someone who can articulate thoughts with clarity and confidence is one I cherish deeply. I am grateful for the invaluable lessons learned, the relationships built, and the personal growth achieved. As I look to the future, I carry with me not just the skills that I have honed but also a newfound appreciation for the power of effective communication and the profound impact it can have on both a personal and professional level. This journey may have reached a significant milestone, but it is far from over. The skills I have gained will continue to serve me as I navigate new opportunities and challenges. As the saying goes, “once a Toastmaster, always a Toastmaster,” and I wholeheartedly embrace this mantra as I step forward, ready to take on whatever comes next.

Sivaramakrishnan Elumalai
Secretary
