One Degree - A Short Toastmasters Story

About 1.5 years ago, I finally decided to face something I had avoided for years - joining Toastmasters. I always knew about the organization, but fear held me back. I didn’t know what kind of people I would meet or whether I would ever feel comfortable in such a community. One day, I gathered a bit of courage and searched online for a club where I might feel at home - something with Tamil or Malayalam speakers.

That simple Google search led me to Dubai Tamil Malayalam Toastmasters Club.

Soon after, I received a message from TM Archana, the President at the time. She told me she got my enquiry and kept inviting me to join a meeting as a guest. One evening after work, I finally clicked the link to an online meeting. I thought I would just watch quietly… but Toastmasters had other plans.

I was asked to introduce myself, then suddenly found myself doing Table Topics. And that’s when I met TM Kalpana - confident, warm, and instantly impressive. Something about her energy, and the friendliness of the group, made me feel unexpectedly comfortable. By the end of that meeting, I made a decision I never expected: I joined the club that very same day.

That was my true entry into Toastmasters - not with a big leap, but with one small, brave degree of change.

Anumol Appukuttan
Sergeant At Arms
