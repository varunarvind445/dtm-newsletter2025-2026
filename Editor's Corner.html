A Legacy of Excellence - Celebrating our Trilingual Journey

My Toastmasters journey began long before I ever stepped into a club as an adult. It started with my mother, who introduced me to the world of communication and leadership. Before becoming a Toastmaster, I was a proud Gavelier at the Elite Gavel Club, mentored by the passionate duo TM Ajaykumar and Rajisha. Those early days shaped the foundation of who I am today.

When I turned 18, my mother encouraged me to join Burj Toastmasters Club. Surrounded by talented young speakers, I found myself growing rapidly. But destiny had another plan. A call came from the "Dubai Tamil Malayalam Toastmasters Club, inviting me to take on the role of "Vice President Public Relations". On August 1st, 2021, I stepped into the role with passion, commitment, and a desire to contribute creatively.

Being part of the DTM Club alongside my mother—who served as the VPE—was a unique experience. I wondered whether she would boss over me in the club environment. Instead, I saw a different version of her: professional, encouraging, and respectful. Hearing her call me *Toastmaster Varun* gave me a sense of pride and responsibility.

Over the years, I have created more than 80 flyers, including one that earned the "Phenomenal Poster Award". I explored Canva, Adobe Photoshop, and even experimented with GIFs and video flyers with soundtracks. Creativity became my language, and design became my playground. I also created two newsletters during the COVID period—filled with photos, energy, and positivity—to keep our club connected during challenging times.

Every year, I participate in contests to sharpen my skills and gain new experiences. Winning 3rd place in the International Speech Contest this year was a proud moment that reminded me how far I’ve come.

Today, I stand at the edge of a new chapter:
A fully digital, interactive newsletter for 2025–2026. This time, the newsletter is not just a document—it’s a website. A living, breathing digital space that reflects the spirit of our club, celebrates our members, and showcases our achievements. With interactive elements, modern design, and multilingual support, this newsletter aims to set a new benchmark for creativity in our district.

As I continue this journey, I’m grateful for every mentor, every meeting, every contest, and every moment shared with my mother and my fellow Toastmasters. The Dubai Tamil Malayalam Toastmasters Club has not only shaped my communication skills but also strengthened my identity as a leader, creator, and community contributor.

I hope you enjoy the upcoming digital newsletter as much as I enjoyed creating it.

Varun Arvind
Vice President Public Relations
