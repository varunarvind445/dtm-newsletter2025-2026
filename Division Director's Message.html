Dear Esteemed Members of Dubai Tamil Malayalam Toastmasters Club,

It gives me immense pleasure to connect with you and celebrate the remarkable journey your club continues to undertake.

Your meetings consistently reflect an exceptional standard of quality, well-structured, engaging, and purposeful. It is truly inspiring to witness the level of preparation and professionalism that defines each session. What stands out even more is the energy of your members. The enthusiasm, participation, and positive spirit in the room create an environment that is both motivating and contagious.

Beyond meetings, your club exemplifies something even more special, a true family environment. The warmth, inclusiveness, and mutual support among members make Dubai Tamil Malayalam Toastmasters Club not just a place to learn, but a place to belong.

Your achievements this year are commendable. Growing your membership and reaching an impressive milestone of 9 DCP points is no small feat. It reflects collective commitment, discipline, and a strong vision for excellence.

I would like to extend special appreciation to President TM Kalpana. She is one of the most active and positive Toastmasters I have come across. Her dedication, leadership, and embodiment of Toastmasters values make her an invaluable asset. It is truly a gift for the club to have her at the helm.

Equally, I commend Area Director TM Mahaalakshmi for her role in guiding the club to greater heights. Her experience, wisdom, and knowledge are clearly reflected in the way she mentors, supports, and steers the club toward success.

Adding to your growing list of accomplishments, the club has produced a champion in the name of TM Sivaramakrishnan, fondly known as SRK. He is truly a world champion in the making. It would not be a surprise to see him soon lifting the most coveted World Champion of Public Speaking title. His journey is a reflection of the excellence your club nurtures.

What makes your club even more admirable is your contribution beyond regular Toastmasters activities. The support extended by your team, under the leadership of the President, during the Division F Contest held on 12th April played a crucial role in making the event a grand success. Your willingness to step up and contribute at the division level speaks volumes about your commitment and team spirit.

I profusely thank both the Area Director TM Mahaalakshmi and the President TM Kalpana for this wonderful journey and for the outstanding achievements. The entire club deserves recognition for setting such a high benchmark.

Let us continue to learn, grow, and inspire—together.

With warm regards,

Alwyn Philomen, DTM
Division F Director
